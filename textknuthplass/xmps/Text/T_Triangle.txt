    |                             languages,  and merely means that the pri-

    |                 is  more  or  less interchangeable with ae (not to be|

    |word spellings,  especially for proper names,  may prefer one  or  the|
    |other form (usually _e). Whether to use the umlaut form or the two-let-
    |ter form is usually an arbitrary  choice  in  electronic  typesetting,|
